### Additional Questions (Common Devpost prompts)
**Q: How does your project specifically address the AI × Healthcare theme?**
*A: PharmaGuard AI targets the widespread clinical risk of Adverse Drug Events (ADEs) in polypharmacy patients. It pairs deterministic pharmacological knowledge graphs with multimodal parsing to detect life-threatening drug contraindications, intelligently space conflicting doses into a zero-overlap timeline, and draft physician-ready SBAR memos.*

**Q: Did you build this during the hackathon period?**
*A: Yes. All code, interfaces, algorithms, and test benchmarks were conceived and implemented during the DSH Hacks V2 competition window.*

**Q: Are there any hardware or external dependencies required?**
*A: No. PharmaGuard AI runs standalone with standard web browsers and standard Python 3, making it deployable on low-cost devices and accessible to resource-constrained clinics.*
