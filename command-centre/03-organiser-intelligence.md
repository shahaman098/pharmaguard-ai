## 1. Organiser Landscape & Deep Background

### Organiser 1: STEMise
- **Status:** Verified.
- **Background:** High-impact student-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit (fiscally sponsored by Hack Club / The Hack Foundation) dedicated to expanding accessible STEM education, hackathons, and technical workshops to thousands of youth globally.
- **Values:** Real-world utility, educational empowerment, low barrier to entry, clean execution, and demonstrable technical skills.

### Organiser 2: DeltaForge Hacks & NXT Horizon
- **Status:** Verified.
- **Background:** Youth-led developer organizations focused on hackathon organizing, technical accelerators, and AI/ML project incubation.
- **Series Evolution:** DSH Hacks V1 focused on AI × STEM education (math/learning tools). V2 intentionally elevated the stakes by challenging students to build **AI × Healthcare** technical products that bridge clinical and patient realities.
