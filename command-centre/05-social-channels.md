## 2. Community Intelligence Observations
- **Submission Crunch:** Like most student hackathons, 80%+ of submissions arrive in the final 3 hours before cutoff.
- **Common Blockers:** Video rendering delays, YouTube upload processing lags, and missing one-page PDF attachments.
- **Strategic Rule:** Submit initial version 2 hours early; Devpost permits non-destructive updates up until the deadline lock.
