## 2. Top-Three In-Depth Comparison

### Finalist 1 (Recommended Primary): **PharmaGuard AI**
- *Why it wins:* Solves an undeniable clinical tragedy (1.3M annual ER visits); demonstrates deterministic pharmacology (no hallucinations); features a visible 30-second wow factor (interactive schedule de-confliction); exports real SBAR notes; backed by 12 automated unit tests.

### Finalist 2 (Fallback): **MedEcho Scribe**
- *Why strong:* Highly empathetic dual-perspective interface (doctor view vs. patient view).
- *Downside:* Audio transcription and simulated audio streaming introduce demo latency during short video screen recording.

### Finalist 3: **DermLens AI**
- *Why strong:* Highly visual image-based AI with immediate visual appeal.
- *Downside:* Computer vision classification of skin lesions faces clinical liability scrutiny from medical judges without clinical trial data.
