# 02 — Rules and Compliance Matrix: DSH Hacks V2

---

## 1. Official Participation Rules & Eligibility
- **Age & Status:** Open globally to students aged **13 and older** who are enrolled in an educational institution (middle school, high school, undergraduate, or graduate). Companies and professional commercial entities are strictly excluded.
- **Team Size:** Individual (solo) or teams up to 4 members.
- **Fresh Code Requirement:** All core code must be created during the official competition window. Pre-existing proprietary platforms are prohibited.
- **AI Policy:** Using AI tools (LLMs, code generation assistants, APIs) is **explicitly permitted and encouraged** to build solutions and lower barriers.
- **Intellectual Property:** Participants retain 100% full ownership of their code, IP, and project assets (standard Devpost terms).
