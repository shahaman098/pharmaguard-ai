1. The Clinical Problem & Gap

1.3M+ Emergency Room Visits: Adverse Drug Events (ADEs) represent a leading cause of preventable hospitalizations, heavily concentrated in polypharmacy patients taking 5+ daily prescriptions across disconnected providers.

  • Pharmacokinetic Blindspots: Dangerous drug-drug interactions (e.g., CYP3A4 inhibition) and food-drug chelation (e.g., calcium blocking antibiotics) are buried in fine-print leaflets.
  • Lack of Actionable Triage: Existing tools provide raw warnings without rescheduling conflicted doses or giving doctors structured escalation summaries.

2. PharmaGuard's Novel Solution

PharmaGuard closes the last-mile clinical safety gap by pairing a deterministic pharmacology knowledge engine with an automated scheduling solver and physician communication pipeline:

  • Multi-Tier Severity Engine: Distinguishes absolute contraindications from temporal absorption conflicts with 100% verified recall.
  • Autonomous Schedule De-confliction: Automatically computes a 24-hour administration timeline separating chelating agents by safe windows.
  • Standardized SBAR Escalation: Auto-drafts clinician-ready memos for pharmacists and primary care physicians.

3. End-to-End System Architecture & Workflow

Step 1: Intake Multimodal vision/text parsing of prescription labels & OTC supplements.
Step 2: Rule Engine Cross-reference against RxNorm & FDA FAERS pharmacological interaction matrix.
Step 3: Temporal Solver Calculates pharmacokinetic spacing windows to eliminate chelation clashes.
Step 4: Clinical SBAR Generates formatted Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation note.
Step 5: Patient Care Card Plain-English 5th-grade reading level daily schedule & audio alerts.
