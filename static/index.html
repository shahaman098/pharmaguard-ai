What we check

  • Dangerous pairs: one medicine raising another’s level, bleeding, heart rhythm, high potassium, serotonin overload.
  • Blocking: calcium, iron and antacids that stop other medicines being absorbed.
  • Timing: empty stomach, with food, bedtime, twice a day, weekly and as-needed.
  • Honesty: anything outside our 52-medicine list is flagged as not checked.
