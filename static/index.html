What the engine checks

  • Interactions: CYP450, renal clearance, pharmacodynamic synergy, QT, bleeding, hyperkalemia.
  • Absorption: calcium/iron chelation and thyroid-hormone spacing.
  • Timing: fasting, meals, bedtime, twice-daily intervals, weekly and as-needed medicines.
  • Fail-safe: anything outside the 52-drug demo knowledge base is marked unverified.
